# Alternative Parts — Open-Humanoid-180

> **Status:** work in progress.
>
> Budget‑friendly substitutes and fallback components in case primary parts become unavailable.

## Planned Content

- Replacements for 6384 / 5065 / 3205 motors (different KV, other brands).
- Alternatives to ODrive3.6 (VESC, MKS XDrive, DIY).
- Hoverboard ESC substitutes (other FOC‑capable ESCs).
- Alternative encoders (AS5047, TLE5012 as MT6826S replacements).
- Material alternatives: PA12‑CF → PETG+CF, aluminium → steel / printed parts.
- Where to source cheap components (scavenging from robot vacuums, e‑scooters).

## Status

This document will be updated as:
- Alternative components are tested.
- Supply shortages or high prices are identified for primary parts.
